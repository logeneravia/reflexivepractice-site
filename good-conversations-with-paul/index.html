These are conversations for good — not merely pleasant conversations, but conversations that do good. Conversations that strengthen communities, deepen understanding, and remind us that disagreement need not mean division, but an invitation to curiosity and exploration.

Good Conversations Episode 1 — Erin Peden

We do not become ourselves in isolation. Our identities are shaped in relationship — by the people we associate with, the places we inhabit, the things we cherish, the ideas we explore, and the actions we take. In this first conversation, Paul sits down with Erin Peden to explore what it means to be truly present in dialogue — and what becomes possible when we listen with curiosity and courage.
