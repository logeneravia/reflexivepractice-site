Inspiration

The day when you wake in the morning and your first thought is, ‘Huzzah, this one is going to be awesome!

Some days the thing you need to say to yourself the most is, ‘it’s okay to take a break from life and have a nap.’

Finding a personal balance between caring for one’s self and caring for others is one of the most complicated and intriguing conundrums of complex modern life – particularly in a world that’s full of constantly shifting relationships which continue to influence our identities.

The space in between is where we work together to create meaning.  To be changed through our meaning-making we must practice ways of engaging in our partnership or co-creators.

Every conversation changes the future in some way.

Friends make a difference.

It’s only the future that can judge the past. Be very careful what you create in the present.

If you want to know where your heart is, pay attention to where your mind goes when it wanders off.
