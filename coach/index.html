Paul Harris

… is passionate about personal and community transformation and seeks to understand the complex issues that interconnect all of us. His theories about identity and transformation have helped individuals and organisations discover ways to navigate and harness the complexity of the world and have helped them find paths to better futures.

His belief is that our relationships with people, places, ideas, activities, and things are the most powerful influences on our decision making, but that we are never without power to change all of these in ways to realise our deepest ambitions, hopes, and aspirations for the future.

For several years now Paul Harris has worked as a mentor with individuals seeking control and agency in their professional and personal lives. Paul’s unique holistic approach opens up possibilities for deep change for individuals and organisation for sustainable growth in all these disparate areas of work.

He is a well-known community and business leader, based in Red Deer, Alberta, who has many years of success in directing and nurturing successful retail and hospitality businesses, non-profit organisations and in local government. With experience at national and international levels, Paul has synthesised his postgraduate study in Canada, USA, and Europe, with innovative development practice across the fields of personal growth, community development, the arts, and organisational innovation.
