To achieve our best future we must become skilled at getting out of our own way.

Paul Harris, Reflexive Practitioner

Reflexive Practice is a way of being in the world that invites thoughtful framing of all relationships to create meaning that is generative and life-giving.

Relationships define us, hold us, shape us, and transform us. They exist with everything around us – the people we bump against daily, the activities we participate in, the things we think about, and the places we go.

It is a wonder that we feel we have any ability to be our own person in this chaotic and influential world.

The greatest shift we can make in our thinking is to deeply realise that we too can transform, shape, and define everything around us in exactly the way we are influenced.

To harness our greatest strength is to become a practitioner of self, to learn how to frame, reframe, and create artefacts that draws a better life toward us with every waking moment.
